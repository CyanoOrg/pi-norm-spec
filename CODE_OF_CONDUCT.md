# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe. Be direct
about code and design while remaining respectful toward people. Assume good
faith, provide evidence, accept correction, and make room for contributors with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `bravetwo@163.com`. Include relevant links
or context, but do not investigate or publish private information yourself.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban accounts in
proportion to the behavior and its impact. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
